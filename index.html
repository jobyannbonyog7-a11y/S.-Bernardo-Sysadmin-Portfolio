Roles & Responsibilities

System Administrators are the guardians of IT infrastructure. Here are their core responsibilities:

Installing & Maintaining OS

Deploy, configure, and maintain operating systems (Windows Server, Linux distributions) ensuring optimal performance, security patches, and system updates across the infrastructure.

Managing User Accounts

Create, modify, and delete user accounts. Manage permissions, access controls, group policies, and enforce security best practices for all system users.

Monitoring System Performance

Continuously monitor CPU, memory, disk, and network usage. Identify bottlenecks, optimize resources, and ensure high availability of critical services.

Backups & Recovery

Design and implement robust backup strategies. Perform regular backups, test recovery procedures, and ensure business continuity in case of data loss or disasters.

Troubleshooting Issues

Diagnose and resolve hardware, software, and network problems. Provide rapid response to incidents, minimize downtime, and document solutions for future reference.

Security Management

Implement firewalls, antivirus, intrusion detection systems. Apply security patches, manage certificates, and protect systems against evolving cyber threats.
